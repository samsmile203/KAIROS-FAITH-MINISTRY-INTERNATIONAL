About Us

Serving God, building lives, and reaching the world with hope.

We are a vibrant church family committed to spiritual growth, worship, prayer, and the transformation of lives through the power of God’s Word.

Vision and Mission

Our Vision

We exist to make disciples of Jesus Christ, help them discover their God given purpose, deepen their faith, and experience the peace of God in a welcoming and uplifting environment.

Our Mission

  • To empower believers by the preaching of the Word of faith.(Hebrews 11:6)
  • To build up a generation of believers who undertands times and seasons.(1 Chronicles 12:32).
  • To raise leaders for the body of Christ.(2 Timothy 2:2)
  • To bring people to the foreknowledge of the ministry of the Holy Spirit.(John 16:13-15)
  • To extend helping hand to the needy in the society.(Matthew 25:36)
