Welcome to Kairos Faith Ministry International

Where Lives Are Transformed

Welcome to Kairos Faith Ministry International (KFMI), a believing charismatic church where the Word of God is taught with accuracy, and the power of the Holy Spirit is demonstrated.

Kairos Faith Ministry International was founded in 2025 by Rev. Isaac Kansan and Lady Mrs. Sarah Kansan with a passion to see lives transformed through the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

Our vision is to make disciples of Christ who will positively influence every sphere of life, bringing the light of the Gospel into families, education, media, government, business, arts and entertainment, and other areas of society.

Located in Koforidua - Okorase, KFMI is committed to raising believers who know the Word, walk by faith, and demonstrate the life and power of the Holy Spirit.

As we grow, we look forward to planting daughter churches in the near future, extending the Gospel and impacting communities for Christ.
